Cybersecurity Awareness Hub

Learn the fundamentals to protect yourself and your organization.

Phishing

Phishing is an attack where scammers disguise themselves as a trustworthy entity to steal sensitive information like passwords or credit card numbers.

How to spot it:
  • Urgent or threatening language.
  • Unexpected requests for sensitive information.
  • Mismatched or suspicious sender email addresses.

Password Security & 2FA

Weak passwords are the easiest way for hackers to access your accounts. Always use unique, complex passwords for every service.

Best Practices:
  • Use a Password Manager.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
  • Never reuse passwords across different sites.

Social Engineering

Social engineering is the psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information.

Prevention:
  • Verify the identity of anyone asking for sensitive data.
  • Don't blindly trust individuals claiming authority (e.g., "IT Support" calling you).

Malware & Ransomware

Malicious software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access. Ransomware encrypts your files and demands payment.

Protection:
  • Keep your operating system and antivirus updated.
  • Do not download software from untrusted sources.
  • Regularly backup your important files offline.

Public Wi-Fi Risks

Free Wi-Fi networks in cafes or airports are often unencrypted, meaning attackers can intercept the data you send and receive.

Safety measures:
  • Use a Virtual Private Network (VPN).
  • Avoid accessing banking or sensitive apps on public networks.

Safe Browsing & Data Privacy

Browsing safely means protecting your personal data and ensuring the sites you visit are secure.

Guidelines:
  • Always check for HTTPS in the URL.
  • Limit the personal information you share on social media.
  • Be cautious of clicking random links or pop-ups.
